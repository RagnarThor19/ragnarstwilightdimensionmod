Drop the three track files in THIS folder, named exactly:

    twilight_1.ogg
    twilight_2.ogg
    twilight_3.ogg

Format
------
* OGG Vorbis (not .mp3, not .wav, and not OGG Opus - Minecraft only decodes Vorbis).
* Stereo is fine here, and is what you want. Mono is only required for *positional*
  sounds, the kind that get quieter as you walk away from them. Dimension music is
  played non-positionally, straight into the Music channel, so it never attenuates.
  Vanilla's own music tracks are all stereo. Mono files will still work, they will
  just sound narrower.
* Any normal sample rate is fine (44.1 kHz or 48 kHz).

Once the files are in place they are picked up automatically - the sound events are
already declared in assets/ragnarstwilightdimension/sounds.json. Until then the game
logs a harmless "file missing for sound event" warning on startup.

Testing a single track without travelling to the dimension:

    /playsound ragnarstwilightdimension:music.twilight_1 music @s

This folder can be deleted from version control once the real files are added; the
placeholder only exists so the directory survives an empty checkout.
